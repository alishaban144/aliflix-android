Alessandro B @RT24719186 Aug 16, 2026 In each episode, the protagonists react emotionally and vengefully to their circumstances, in ways that are unacceptable and condemnable by any civil, moral, or ethical standard. Their reactions are spiteful and vindictive, yet if the world were ending tomorrow and morality no longer mattered, these are precisely the reactions we'd love to indulge in, which is why we empathize with the protagonists and relish watching them pursue their deranged vendettas, made only funnier when they are clumsily undone by the very schemes they set in motion.

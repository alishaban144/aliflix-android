## Native Gemini Live

Talk uses only `gemini-3.8-live`, pinned to `Aoede`. Fish, Groq, separate ASR/TTS and Android voice fallbacks are removed from this feature. Existing recommendation modes are unchanged. Android streams 16 kHz mono PCM directly to Google's constrained Live WebSocket and renders native 24 kHz PCM. Native automatic turn detection, interruption, transcriptions, context compression and session resumption are enabled. Android supplies communication audio routing, audio focus and acoustic echo cancellation where supported. No bundled model is added.

Cloudflare authenticates existing signed-in/temporary identities and issues one-use, short-lived tokens with constrained model, voice, instructions, tools and output budgets. Permanent keys remain encrypted server secrets. Only a resumption handle may change. Reconnects retain the same model and voice; exhausted recovery shows retry. Late callbacks and cancelled tool calls cannot start a stale playback generation. New AudioTracks reset their sample accounting, and interruption flushes are synchronized with writes.

SQLite state contains bounded private session metadata and licensed public evidence; no conversation PCM or transcript is stored by Cloudflare. Optional local history is off by default, owner-fenced, excluded from backup and bounded to twelve turns. Delete history clears local context, resumption handles and server owner state. Tokens expire after twenty minutes; new sessions must start within sixty seconds. Native context compression starts at 8192 tokens and targets 4096. Daily mint quotas are twelve/IP and forty globally; actual Tavily calls are limited to two/session and twenty globally/day. Each session allows twelve uncached knowledge requests. Cache hits and successful source lookups do not spend search quota.

## Film and episode knowledge

Ending/death/twist questions authorize that requested title or episode without a consent toggle or spoken confirmation. Follow-ups retain title/year and distinguish events from interpretation. Exact episode questions never authorize later episodes. A viewer's own completed ranges remain gaps when episodes were skipped; an unrelated series cannot inherit its permissions. No timestamp-level scene boundary is asserted without reliable timed evidence.

Wikipedia now uses a fixed revision fetched through the revision API before parsing plot/interpretation sections. Wikidata validates film/year/IMDb identity; TVMaze validates the exact show/season/episode. Bounded Tavily retrieval is used only when direct evidence is insufficient, with source URL, licence and exact identity checks. Only Wikipedia/TVMaze licensed content is accepted from search; arbitrary snippets and TMDB prose are excluded. Sources are attached discreetly to the optional transcript. The first detailed plot explanation verifies specific events once and reuses evidence across follow-ups. Retrieval outages do not automatically refuse familiar films; uncertain fine detail should be omitted.

## Playback, ratings and mobile design

Filmo remains first for movies, Serienstream for series, AniWorld for Japanese anime. Exhausted/unavailable/network/parser failures now permit bounded automatic recovery. The source picker exposes providers only; internal mirrors are tried automatically. Explicit user choices and native playback controls remain available.

The inspected Filmo Shining page currently exposes Deutsch VOE/Byse; actual VOE metadata declares only German, despite a generic multi_audio flag. Its master has no English audio rendition. The app must not fabricate an English track. A silent, paused Media3 inspection can offer a verified original-language edition from another provider, preserving title, position, selected captions, manual delay and rollback. Both newly resolved and cached startup paths perform the background check. The alternate edition identifies its source honestly.

IMDb matching normalizes Part One / Part I / Pt. 1 / (1), retaining distinct part numbers and exact ID/season/episode checks. Rotten Tomatoes' current episode JSON-LD may omit episodeNumber and part suffixes; the primary series/season plus exact canonical episode URL verifies that case. Episodes with zero critic reviews are NOT_RATED, not assigned invented scores.

The complete Discover Ask card opens Ask Aliflix, including its orb. Season changes animate between exact season contents. Next episode keeps the moving ring, increases its heading, removes Watch credits and its whitespace, and retains an accessible compact countdown-dismiss action.

Talk retains the efficient full-screen native Pixel Flow Field, with a larger reactive orb above, rotating restrained violet glow, clear topic typography below and compact tactile voice/transcript controls. No visible Listening/Thinking labels, chat bubbles, provider names or decorative descriptive paragraphs are added. Reduced motion and minimum touch targets remain supported.

## Validation scope

The physical device is a USB-authorized Pixel 7a. Voice probes inject legally generated questions with the microphone muted and verify actual native Google streaming and physical AudioTrack rendering. They do not prove an unperformed human acoustic barge-in/echo test, subjective voice naturalness, all speakers or all networks. A fixed model/voice identity does not guarantee identical prosody. Preview service availability and third-party streaming catalogue changes remain external constraints.

Initial failed runs are retained privately and are not counted as passes. They exposed an incorrect native model scene detail (fixed by first detailed-plot verification), closing-socket send loss, AudioTrack sample accounting after reconnect, and the cached-route audio check omission. Production speech continues through native automatic VAD; synthetic probes must include a realistic silence tail rather than cutting capture immediately.

Sync with Audio algorithms, confidence gates, original Silero model and ARM64 ONNX/WebRTC binaries remain unchanged. Previously documented short multilingual subtitle-match limits remain in [mobile-168-validation.md](mobile-168-validation.md); this release does not claim those ambiguous cases are solved.

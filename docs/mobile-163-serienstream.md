# Mobile 3.1.163 — Serienstream and coordinated motion

Built from published v3.1.162. Mobile versionCode 253; TV remains 3.1.69/versionCode 159.

## Provider inspection and implementation

- Inspected the actual episode pages and VOE frames for Dark (2017), Pantheon and 1899, including their language link attributes, host redirects, player configuration, HLS masters, captions and audio metadata.
- Serienstream currently exposes a series catalogue. Movies continue through the existing movie providers. Its separately linked Filmo website is not silently substituted for Serienstream.
- The tested Dark S1E1 page lists German. Pantheon and 1899 S1E1 list German and English as separate video editions. These are not HLS audio renditions that can be merged into a single download. Switching editions preserves episode identity and playback position. The tested VOE masters had one muxed audio stream and no captions. Available host captions and actual HLS audio/subtitle groups remain supported when a host supplies them.
- Pantheon's tested German edition was 25 fps; its English edition was 24 fps. Subtitle selection and correction still use the active source and player clock.
- Resolve the direct title route first and verify IMDb identity, or exact title and first release year when IMDb is unavailable. Search is a fallback: broad searches such as Dark include recently added titles with the word in their descriptions and can omit the exact series from the first page.
- Request the exact season and episode. Only inspected VOE language links are used. Doodstream and other host links were observed but are not advertised as verified native adapters.
- Preserve the host's HLS master, headers, source identity and saved playback route. German-original series automatically route to Serienstream, including when another general default is configured; explicit per-title provider choices remain available. Serienstream is in the initial general series queue and download queue, and movies do not waste an attempt on its series-only catalogue.
- Decode only the public VOE player configuration. Downloaded host JavaScript is never evaluated by the native resolver. HTTP redirects are bounded, cancellable and retain cookies only on their own origin.
- Serienstream's redirect gate changed during live testing. Its gates can apply to individual video redirects, so cookies alone do not guarantee a direct HTTP path. A bounded browser step now loads the real selected episode, runs the site's unmodified security scripts and continues only after the site has issued a successful response. It never clicks a challenge or creates a response token. Interactive checks remain visible through the provider card when necessary. Android omits redirected URLs from shouldInterceptRequest, so the approved VOE document is also observed through redirect and resource callbacks. This preserves episode, audio and position and immediately returns ownership to native playback.
- Playback, audio editions and downloads share the provider's WebView cookie store, with explicit disk persistence and consistent user agent. Approved exact-episode/language frames are cached for ten minutes across app restarts; cookies keep the site's own expiry. Ordinary automatic checks run during preparation without requiring the verification window on every use. The website can still request interaction when its session expires or its policy changes.

## UI

- A shared launch clock assembles three staggered panels of the original Aliflix mark, settles the wordmark and reveals Movies, Series and Stories. The complete animation and Home dissolve budget is approximately 1.8 seconds; network waiting cannot extend the branded phase. Home composes during the final still hold. Reduced-motion and lifecycle pause behavior remain supported.
- My List keeps its active genre tab visible and centers it where space permits, with a damped spring. Tab selection still scrolls to the matching section.
- Subtitle Language becomes Preferred language. Settings selection sheets use the existing surfaces, spacing and accent tokens. The Get in touch card opens an ACTION_SENDTO email intent addressed to Aliflixapp@proton.me, with an email-app fallback message.
- Serienstream language editions appear through the player's subtitle shortcut, while advanced sync controls remain in More. Download audio selection uses a modern sheet and marks the original language. Separate editions are downloaded individually; All tracks remains available for true multiaudio masters.
